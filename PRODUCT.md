# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Office staff and admins at Cignes Academy. They aren't technical. Day to day they set up courses and their curricula (modules, topics, lessons), run batches, register students, record payments, and follow up enquiries from the website. They work at a desk, often switching between several tasks, and come back to the same screens many times a day.

## Product Purpose

The academy's admin panel. It keeps courses, batches, students, payments and enquiries in one place, against the same backend as the Cignes CRM, and feeds the public website (course pages read their curriculum from here). Success is staff finishing a task quickly and correctly, without wondering where to look or what a control does.

## Brand Personality

Calm, orderly, dependable. Quiet screens with a clear hierarchy and a guided reading order, so each screen has one obvious place to start. The tone of labels and messages is plain and direct.

## Anti-references

- Busy dashboards with many equal-weight tiles or cards competing for attention ("I don't know where to look").
- Nested card-in-card-in-card layouts.
- Decorative color, gradients and glass effects that carry no meaning.
- Anything that stops looking like the Cignes CRM; this app deliberately shares its design system.

## Design Principles

1. **One focal point per screen.** Lead with the thing the person came to do; everything else steps back.
2. **Structure shows the hierarchy.** Nesting, indentation and weight tell you what belongs to what, not boxes around every item.
3. **Quiet until needed.** Secondary actions stay out of the way until you reach for them; primary actions are obvious.
4. **Match the CRM.** Same tokens, components and density as the Cignes CRM, so the two feel like one product.
5. **Plain words.** Labels and messages say what happens, in the staff's own terms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), full keyboard access with visible focus, and reduced-motion alternatives for any animation. Light and dark themes are both supported.
